# Automattic

Automattic is the company associated with WordPress.com and several WordPress ecosystem products. In [[wordpress-everywhere]], [[matt-mullenweg]] uses Automattic and WordPress.com as part of the long-term trust argument around WordPress, including exportability, low lock-in, permalink stewardship, and 100-year plans.

## Role

In this vault, Automattic currently matters as institutional context for [[wordpress]] and [[my-wordpress-net]]. The source argues that long-term stewardship will matter more in an AI age where it is easy to generate software but harder to trust that software, its data, and its URLs will endure.

# Alejandro Piad Morffis

Alejandro Piad Morffis is the author of [[ai-is-doing-something-weird-to-science]], published in Mostly Harmless AI. In this source, he argues that AI-assisted science should be analyzed as a loop of posing, proposing, verifying, and curating rather than as a simple question of whether a model "did" the science.

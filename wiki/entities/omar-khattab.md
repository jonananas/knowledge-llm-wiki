# Omar Khattab

Omar Khattab is an MIT CSAIL researcher ([homepage](https://omarkhattab.com/)) and a co-author of [[sparks-of-weight-space-programming]]. That post argues that LLMs can optimize neural network weights directly, beyond the text artifacts they already optimize, such as prompts and harnesses.

# Armin Ronacher

Armin Ronacher is a software developer and the author of [[astra-for-coding-why-are-we-doing-this-again]]. In that article, he draws on experience working with CPython and reports a long-running [[gpt-6-astra]] coding experiment focused on Python virtual threads and lexical scoping.

His contribution to this vault is a practitioner critique of autonomy-heavy AI coding. He distinguishes impressive general capability from fitness for human-maintained software and argues that task completion and token efficiency can conflict with readability, architectural coherence, and trust.

# Observable

Observable is a data-visualization and data-app platform associated with Observable notebooks, Observable Plot, D3, and [[observable-framework]]. In the [[observable-2-0]] announcement, Observable positions its 2.0 direction around moving beyond notebooks into code-first [[data-apps]], dashboards, and reports.

## Role

For this vault, Observable matters as a developer-tooling company focused on communicating with data. The Observable 2.0 announcement draws a clear product boundary: notebooks remain useful for exploration, but durable team-facing dashboards should be built with workflows closer to normal software development.

Observable's hosted platform is also part of the story: [[observable-framework]] projects can be self-hosted or deployed to Observable for team sharing. [[observable-2-0]]

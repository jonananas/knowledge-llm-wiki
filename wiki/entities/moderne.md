# Moderne

Moderne is a developer-tools company focused on large-scale code remediation and automated refactoring. In the [[moderne-ai-code-search-at-scale]] source, Moderne describes an AI-powered code-search workflow built on [[openrewrite]] recipes for finding method invocations from natural-language descriptions.

## Role

Moderne matters in this vault as an example of AI applied to codebases through deterministic program-analysis infrastructure. Its approach in the source is not free-form agentic editing; it narrows AI work through method signatures, method patterns, top-k selection, model chaining, and generated recipes.

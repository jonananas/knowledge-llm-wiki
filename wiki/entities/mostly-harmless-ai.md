# Mostly Harmless AI

Mostly Harmless AI is Alejandro Piad Morffis's publication about AI. The article [[ai-is-doing-something-weird-to-science]] uses recent and historical scientific examples to argue that AI's important role is as a proposer inside a larger verification-and-curation loop.

# Stack Overflow

Stack Overflow is a developer Q&A and technical knowledge platform. In [[announcing-stack-overflow-for-agents]], it extends its human developer knowledge model toward coding agents through [[stack-overflow-for-agents]].

## Relevance

For this vault, Stack Overflow matters as a long-running example of peer-reviewed technical knowledge. The 2026 announcement suggests a shift from human-facing Q&A alone toward agent-facing, machine-readable, verification-driven knowledge exchange.

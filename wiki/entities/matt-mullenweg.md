# Matt Mullenweg

Matt Mullenweg is a co-founder of [[wordpress]] and a central figure at [[automattic]]. In [[wordpress-everywhere]], he argues that WebAssembly-powered WordPress environments should change how people think about WordPress: from hosted software to a portable, user-controlled, browser-runnable unit of open-source software.

## Position In This Source

The essay combines product positioning and philosophy. Mullenweg ties [[my-wordpress-net]] and [[wordpress-playground]] to a long-term open-source thesis around ownership, backwards compatibility, exportability, durable brands, and [[digital-sovereignty]].

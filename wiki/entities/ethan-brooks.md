# Ethan Brooks

Ethan Brooks is the author of The Write to Roam. In [[how-to-write-stuff-no-one-else-can]], he argues that writers can build a moat by finding overlooked people and sources with direct experience rather than competing over the same public material.

## Research Style

The article emphasizes manual, context-building research: reconstruct a company timeline, map employees around turning points, identify behind-the-scenes contributors, and use targeted conversations to reach information that is not already circulating publicly.

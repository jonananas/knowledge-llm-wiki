# Neo4j

Neo4j is a graph database company. In this vault, it currently appears as the publisher of the [[graphrag-pattern-catalog]], a CC BY 4.0 reference site for graph-backed [[retrieval-augmented-generation]] patterns. [[graphrag-com]]

## Relevance

Neo4j matters here because the GraphRAG catalog frames graph databases as a practical substrate for representing document chunks, entities, relationships, community summaries, domain facts, and retrieval paths. The catalog's examples and patterns often use Cypher, Neo4j Vector retrieval, and Neo4j-related tooling.

# Information Advantage

Information advantage is a competitive edge created by knowing useful things that others do not. In writing and research, [[how-to-write-stuff-no-one-else-can]] frames it as a more cultivable and AI-resistant advantage than money or unusual personal behavior.

## In Writing

The article's core claim is that the best writing moat is often access to people with direct experience and little public attention. Famous figures attract the most inbound requests, but the surrounding collaborators may have richer operational insight and are often easier to reach.

## How To Build It

- Look behind the public face of a company, book, project, or event.
- Identify people close to the work but outside the attention spotlight.
- Use neglected sources: old newspapers, archives, niche communities, public records, podcasts, and interviews.
- Convert one strong source into a network by asking who else should be consulted.

# Interview Chaining

Interview chaining is a source-discovery technique where each conversation is used to identify the next useful person to contact. In [[how-to-write-stuff-no-one-else-can]], Brooks highlights a simple closing question: ask who else should be consulted about the topic.

## Why It Works

People close to a domain know the informal map of who did the work, who remembers the details, and who has context that public records miss. A single well-placed source can therefore reveal a better source network than search alone.

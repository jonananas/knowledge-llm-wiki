# Cloud Development Environments

Cloud development environments are development workspaces that run remotely rather than directly on a developer's local machine. They can provide reproducible setup, fast onboarding, shared environments, browser access, and centralized compute.

The [[codesandbox-homepage]] source presents [[codesandbox]] as a cloud development environment platform focused on isolated sandboxes that can be created, resumed, cloned, and hibernated programmatically.

## Why They Matter

- They reduce local setup burden for developers, students, and reviewers.
- They can make ephemeral environments practical for demos, education, support, and CI workflows.
- They create a natural execution layer for AI agents that need a place to inspect, edit, run, and test code.
- They can centralize policy, isolation, and lifecycle control around development compute.
